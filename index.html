At Nexabytes Studio, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Gem Match collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Gem Match is designed as a casual puzzle game. We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or contacts) directly on our servers.

A. Gameplay & Local Storage Data

  • Game Progress: Unlocked levels, high scores, earned stars, coin balance, and owned boosters (Hammer, Shuffle, +5 Moves).
  • User Preferences: Sound effects, haptic feedback toggle, hint indicators, and animation settings.
  • Storage Location: All gameplay state is stored locally on your device via standard client-side storage mechanisms and is not uploaded to external private databases.

B. Network Connection & Automated Technical Data

  • Active Network Requirement: Gem Match requires an active internet connection to authenticate game state, deliver level assets, and serve advertisements. If your device goes offline, gameplay will prompt you to reconnect.
  • Log & Diagnostic Data: In case of technical errors or crashes, standard anonymized diagnostics (operating system version, screen resolution, device model) may be automatically processed to ensure app stability.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gem Match utilizes Google AdMob to display Banner ads, Interstitial ads (shown after level completion), and opt-in Rewarded Video ads (providing extra moves, coins, or boosters).

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device-specific information to serve relevant ads, limit ad frequency, and measure ad performance. This may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • IP address (used to infer coarse geographic region).
  • Interaction events with advertisements.

For detailed information on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy at:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Information

The information processed is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver and maintain seamless puzzle gameplay across 1,000 procedural levels.
  • Provide opt-in rewards (coins, extra moves, power-ups) through rewarded video ads.
  • Verify network connectivity so advertisements and game features function properly.
  • Improve game stability, detect bugs, and enhance user experience.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Gem Match is a general-audience casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Our ad requests are configured in compliance with Google Play's Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at ikbalsiddiki45@gmail.com and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

5. GDPR and California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Union or California), you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Deletion: You can clear all game data at any time directly in the in-game Settings screen by tapping "Reset Game Progress", or by clearing App Data in your device settings.
  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based ads through your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard application data. All local game storage is sandboxed within your application container, and network requests utilize standard encrypted HTTPS protocols.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage players to review this policy periodically.
